Seed scenario (System 1 builds seed/demo.json ; DEMO_TODAY env sets the date, all other dates are relative) Business "Maya Studio", USD, starting cash $9,800, reserve $5,000. Obligations: payroll $6,800 on today+4, supplier $1,400 on today+7, rent $2,200 on today+10. Six payers, one per behaviour: two reliable , one slow , two chronic , one ghost . About 14 invoices, 8 overdue totaling roughly $14,000 to $15,000; the three largest overdue invoices belong to the slow and chronic payers. Calibration rule: the baseline forecast must breach the reserve at payroll with a shortfall of roughly $1,500 to $2,500, and collecting any two of the three largest overdue invoices must close the gap. Persona behaviour (used by the payer simulator): reliable pays within 2 days of a reminder; slow pays when offered a discount of 2% or more; chronic accepts instalments and ignores reminders; ghost never pays, so the agent escalates. Policy defaults: maxDiscountPct 5, maxInstalments 3, autoApproveMaxAmount $500, approval required for discounts, instalments and escalation, quiet hours 20:00 to 08:00, at most 2 reminders per invoice per week.
